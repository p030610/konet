# Audio Attribution

The driving speech audio in this dataset derives from the nine openly licensed public speech corpora listed below. Sources under CC BY (3.0 / 4.0) require attribution; CC0 sources are credited as a courtesy. Clip counts refer to the 11,255 released clips.

**Modifications**: the source audio was segmented (~10 s excerpts), re-encoded (AAC), and muxed with AI-generated video. The speech content itself was not otherwise altered.

| corpus | clips | license | source |
|---|--:|---|---|
| Multilingual LibriSpeech (OpenSLR 94) | 4,107 | CC BY 4.0 | https://www.openslr.org/94/ |
| The People's Speech (MLCommons) | 3,521 | CC BY (2.0–4.0) | https://huggingface.co/datasets/MLCommons/peoples_speech |
| VoxPopuli (Meta) | 1,237 | CC0 1.0 | https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/voxpopuli |
| LibriSpeech (OpenSLR 12) | 846 | CC BY 4.0 | https://www.openslr.org/12/ |
| Zeroth-Korean (OpenSLR 40) | 713 | CC BY 4.0 | https://www.openslr.org/40/ |
| FLEURS (Google) | 531 | CC BY 4.0 | https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs |
| YODAS (ESPnet) | 234 | CC BY 3.0 | https://huggingface.co/datasets/espnet/yodas |
| AMI Meeting Corpus (IHM) | 34 | CC BY 4.0 | https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/ |
| MInDS-14 (PolyAI) | 32 | CC BY 4.0 | https://huggingface.co/datasets/PolyAI/minds14 |

Notes:

- **The People's Speech** is distributed by MLCommons in CC BY and CC BY-SA subsets; only the CC BY subset (item-level licenses CC BY 2.0–4.0) is used here. No CC BY-SA or NC material is included in this dataset.
- **YODAS** aggregates Creative Commons–licensed YouTube videos and is redistributed by ESPnet under CC BY 3.0 at the corpus level; we credit it accordingly, following upstream practice.
